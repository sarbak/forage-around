# Why might a tree on the foraging map be missing?

You reach a pin on the map, but the tree you expected is nowhere in sight. A mapped location can be wrong or out of date. Forage Around credits Falling Fruit for its tree locations; Falling Fruit warns that records can contain misplaced markers or old tree-inventory information. Treat the pin as a place to investigate, with room for the visit to turn up nothing. ([Forage Around](https://foragearound.com/), [Falling Fruit’s data guidance](https://fallingfruit.org/about))

The reason starts with how locations reach the map. Falling Fruit combines contributions from people with imported community maps and institutional tree inventories. Those inventories were compiled to document and care for trees, so an entry does not necessarily record a forager finding usable fruit there. ([Imported datasets](https://fallingfruit.org/datasets))

There is also a gap between an original record and a later visit. Falling Fruit says changes made to a source dataset after import are not reflected automatically in its map. A city updating its own inventory therefore does not establish that the corresponding Falling Fruit record has changed. ([How imports work](https://fallingfruit.org/datasets))

When a location does not match what you find, keep the next step small:

- Check that you have reached the mapped place before looking farther afield.
- Keep your observation specific: “I could not find the tree” leaves room for a misplaced pin or a missed plant.
- If you can establish a correction, Falling Fruit invites users to improve its records. ([Falling Fruit](https://fallingfruit.org/about))

The supporting information has a different job. Forage Around’s credits distinguish Falling Fruit locations, Wikipedia and Wikimedia Commons descriptions and photos, and the OpenStreetMap base map. A description or photograph is background information, not verification of the plant at a particular pin. A location or seasonal note does not establish picking permission or ripeness in your region today. ([Data sources and limitations](https://foragearound.com/about))

For planning another visit, the homepage offers an address or place search alongside “Find fruit near me”; choose the option you prefer. ([Search the map](https://foragearound.com/)) Use each location to choose where to look, and let what you can actually confirm determine what happens next.
