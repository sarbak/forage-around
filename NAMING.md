# Naming: Neighborhood foraging + fermentation app

## Decision: Scrump (target domains: getscrump.com / scrump.app — verify & register)

**Working URL:** https://scrump-five.vercel.app (Vercel free alias; custom domain pending)

---

## Why Scrump

**"Scrump"** is the English dialect verb for foraging windfall fruit — gathering the
apples and pears that have dropped (or are about to) from a tree, before the
wildlife gets them. It is the single most precise word in English for exactly what
this app helps you do.

The bonus: **"scrumpy"** is the rough farmhouse cider you ferment from scrumped
apples. The name fuses the two things the product is about — **foraging** and
**fermentation** — in one short, ownable word.

### Online findability (the hard gate)
- **No competing app or AI startup** named Scrump. The foraging-map incumbent is
  "Falling Fruit" (our data source), not Scrump.
- Search noise is benign and non-software: `scrumpy` = West Country cider
  (Wikipedia, Atlas Obscura), and a Lilo & Stitch doll named "Scrump." Neither
  occupies the app/AI namespace.
- `scrumpbot.com` exists as a niche Scrum-meeting dev bot — different word stem in
  practice, different category, no collision.

### Strengths
- One syllable, unambiguous to say and spell once heard in context.
- Semantically exact (forage) **and** thematically exact (ferment → scrumpy).
- Ownable: an uncommon real word, so the brand can own the search namespace the
  way "Venmo" or "Hinge" did.
- Built-in press hook: "the lost art of scrumping, now on your phone."

### Risks / mitigations
- Mild obscurity to US ears ("what's a scrump?") — turn it into the story; the
  definition *is* the tagline.
- Faint "stealing fruit from an orchard" historical connotation — countered by the
  product ethic baked into the UI ("take only what would otherwise fall; knock
  first"). Falling Fruit navigates the same line.
- Bare `scrump.com` likely taken/parked — use `getscrump.com` or `scrump.app`.

## Runner-up evaluated

### Windfall — RED on findability
Semantically perfect (a windfall = fruit blown down from a tree **and** a piece of
unexpected good fortune). Killed by namespace collision: **Windfall Data** (SF
people-intelligence/AI company, $9M+ raised, windfall.com), **Windfall Apps LLC**,
and multiple Google Play apps named WINDFALL. "Windfall app" searches are polluted;
.com and .app unavailable. Great word, unusable namespace.

### Others considered (not advanced)
- **Bough** — pronunciation ambiguity (bow/boff), generic, no foraging specificity.
- **Mast** (forest nut-fall, a real foraging term) — obscure meaning + Mastodon
  client search noise.
- **Gleanly / Glean** — "Glean" is a large enterprise AI-search company. RED.
